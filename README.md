## 📌 Problem Statement
Rapid urbanization has pushed modern metropolises into severe crises:
1. **Unplanned Sprawl & Congestion:** Citizens spend 2+ hours daily commuting due to segregated, car-dependent zoning.
2. **Unequal Civic Access:** Essential facilities (hospitals, schools, courts, rapid transit) are disproportionately located, depriving vulnerable sectors of timely emergency response.
3. **Climate Vulnerability & Heat Islands:** Monolithic concrete surfaces aggravate urban temperatures by 4–8°C and cause catastrophic flash flooding during cloudbursts.
4. **Lack of Predictive Modeling:** Municipal planners lack real-time digital twin simulators to preview urban impacts before billions are spent on physical construction.

---

## 💡 The Solution: UrbanMind AI
**UrbanMind AI** is a flagship generative urban planning studio and digital twin simulator. It empowers city architects, governments, and citizens to co-create sustainable, resilient, and equitable hybrid cities.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Client Custom City Architect:** Define population scale (**1 Million, 100 Million+**), custom territory size in km², and select essential infrastructure (Hospitals, Metro & Transit, Schools, Universities, High Courts, Aerotropolises, Sponge Wetlands, Solar Microgrids, Tech Hubs) with real-time input validation.
- **2D Blueprint & 2.5D Isometric Digital Twin:** Live spatial matrix with volumetric zoning, pedestrian isochrone radiuses, and ambient environmental lighting.
- **12 Individual Sector Visual Galleries:** Curated architectural photography and spatial models across 12 distinct sectors (Healthcare, Transit, Sponge Wetlands, High-Density Housing, Education, Governance, etc.).
- **Interactive Multi-View Workspace:** Seamlessly toggle between **Spatial Matrix**, **Districts Grid**, **Kanban Board**, and **Compact Data Table**.
- **Real-Time Climate & Infrastructure Stress-Testing:** Simulate 100-year cloudburst floods, summer heatwaves, and peak-hour transit shocks with instant KPI updates.
- **AI Urbanist Copilot (Gemini API):** Context-aware generative AI copilot optimizing zoning density, 15-minute walkability, and carbon neutrality.
- **24/7 Helpline & Client Feedback System:** Integrated international toll-free helplines, support emails, headquarters dispatch, and multidimensional feedback rating.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Frontend:** React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Motion (`motion/react`), Lucide React
- **Backend & AI:** Express, Node.js, `@google/genai` (Google Gemini 2.5 Flash / Flash Lite)
- **Design System:** Bento Grid, Layered Glassmorphism, Dynamic Cursor Spotlight, Multi-Theme Engine (Dark/Light + 5 Accent Colors)
